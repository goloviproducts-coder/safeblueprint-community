# Roadmap

This is a direction, not a promise. Dates are not committed. Comment on the linked issue or open a `proposal` issue to shape it. See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) for how decisions are made.

## Now
- **Verify a release:** a public page with copy-paste commands to check checksums and signed attestations.
- **Reproducible builds:** anyone can rebuild the release zip and get the identical file.
- **OpenSSF Best Practices badge:** complete the self-assessment and publish the badge.
- **Community question pack library:** reviewed packs for common sectors, credited to their contributors (see [packs/](packs/)).

## Next
- **Question pack editor page:** a free, open-source page to build and check a pack in the browser, with no upload and no account.
- **More community packs:** further sectors and internal-control templates, reviewed through the pack review process.
- **Security dashboard improvements:** keep the OpenSSF Scorecard at 8 or above.

## Later
- **Hosted team tier:** an optional paid hosted service for teams. The community edition stays free, open source and fully usable on its own.

## Always
- Everything runs on the visitor's device. Nothing a user enters is captured without their consent.
- Not legal advice.
