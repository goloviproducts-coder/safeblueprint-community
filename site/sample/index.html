What the law asks

Australian Privacy Principles (APP) 1.7 to 1.9 start on 10 December 2026.

A privacy policy has to describe some computer-program decisions where personal information is used and the effect on a person can be significant.

Here the program ranks applicants and a recruiter reviews the shortlist. OAIC guidance says a shortlist a person relies on can still count, even when a person reviews it. Source: OAIC APP Guidelines, para 1.41.

APP 1.9: refusing or failing to decide counts, and good or bad effects both count. Screening someone out of a job, or never progressing them, is an effect.

Some applicants may be young people or people experiencing vulnerability, who may be affected more.

The description goes in the APP privacy policy, which the law asks to be kept up to date (APP 1.3).

Good practice, not a legal requirement: OAIC guidance suggests dating the policy and reviewing it regularly, for example yearly or when the program changes. Source: OAIC APP Guidelines, para 1.9.

Exemptions such as small business or employee records are only a screen here. Wattle Lane Retail answered that turnover is over A$3 million. Check exemptions with a lawyer.

OAIC guidance, based on the Explanatory Memorandum rather than the words of APP 1.7, says the policy need not include commercial-in-confidence information or trade secrets, such as how the ranking is weighted. It reads this narrowly: only information whose release could harm the business. Source: OAIC APP Guidelines, para 1.67.

This note is not a finding of compliance.

Suggested wording for a lawyer to edit.

Kinds of personal information. We use applicants' names, contact details, work history and application answers when a computer program does a thing that is substantially and directly related to making this decision: Hiring (AI hiring screen). We do not use sensitive information, such as health information, for this decision.

Substantially and directly related. A computer program ranks applicants for this kind of decision: Hiring. A recruiter reviews the shortlist.
