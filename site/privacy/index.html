Privacy

What stays in the browser, and what is sent only after you agree.

Your answers stay in your browser. We don't store or send them. We never see your initiative names or answers.

What stays on this device

The ADM inventory and the AI security checklist run in your browser. Initiative names, an optional organisation name, reference numbers, timestamps, and answers are kept in memory for this visit only. The organisation name is not stored or sent. Save as PDF, Download HTML, Copy tasks, and Download CSV stay on this device and do not post. Copy tasks leaves out the initiative name. Download CSV includes that name only inside the file you save. The list of decisions from this visit is cleared when you refresh or close the tab.

Playbook request

A playbook request is sent only after you tick "I agree to being emailed the playbook and beta updates. Unsubscribe anytime." It sends your email, a consent flag, which tool, and a fixed subject line. It also sends a short summary of your results only if you tick "Include my checklist results so the playbook is tailored to me". That summary is a verdict and decision type, or an AI score, status tiles, and top three priorities. It does not include your initiative name or organisation name. The form shows the payload under "What we'll send:" before you submit. The browser posts that request only to this site.

Contact form

The contact form sends your name, work email, company, role, company size, what you need, your message, and a consent flag, and only after you tick consent. It does not include checklist answers, inventory answers, or initiative names.

What we do not use

This site does not store answers in local storage, session storage, or cookies, and it does not put them in the page address. We do not use analytics, tracking cookies, or other third-party scripts. In the self-hosted edition the browser makes no network posts at all.

To work offline, your browser keeps a copy of the site's own files. It never stores anything you type.

Deadline

The transparency obligation starts on 10 December 2026. OAIC's fact sheet describes that date and treats "computer program" broadly, including rule-based processes, AI, machine learning, and other software. Source: OAIC fact sheet: APP 1.7–1.9 transparency obligation (PDF).

Decisions that use personal information

From 10 December 2026, an APP entity's privacy policy has to describe certain decisions a computer program makes or substantially shapes, where personal information is used and the decision could significantly affect someone's rights or interests. It applies to APP entities. Many small businesses under A$3 million annual turnover are exempt unless another test applies. This site is a screen, not a finding. Source: OAIC APP Guidelines, Chapter 1 (APP 1).

Infringement notices

Infringement notices of up to about A$21,840 per breach for companies may apply (per OAIC). That is a general figure, not a prediction for you. See OAIC infringement notices. This is not legal advice.

Deletion

To ask for your email to be deleted, use the contact form. We handle that request in line with the Australian Privacy Act.

This privacy note is not legal advice.

Security

How a visit is protected, what was tested on 21 Oct 2026, and how to report a vulnerability are on the security page. To report an issue, use the contact form. Include the page, what you saw, and how to reproduce it. Do not include passwords or customer data. The contact form is the only contact route.

In short

  • Answers stay in this browser.
  • Nothing is sent unless you tick consent.
  • Copy tasks and CSV stay on this device.
  • We never see your initiative names or answers.

To ask for a deletion, use the contact form.
