What is SafeBlueprint

See where your AI decisions really stand, and what to fix first.

SafeBlueprint is a free, open-source check that links each AI or automated decision to one honest portfolio view, then turns the gaps into a short, prioritised plan. Not legal advice.

1. The gap it fills

Often the big picture and the underlying issues don't match. A dashboard says AI is under control. Inside single decisions sit hidden gaps: no human review, vendor AI features nobody listed, missing privacy notices.

  • Why now: from 10 December 2026, APP 1.7 asks organisations to explain in their privacy policy when a computer program makes or substantially informs significant decisions about people.1
  • Adoption is ahead of governance: one survey found 82% of organisations use AI agents but only 44% have policies to secure them.4
  • Big governance platforms are built for large, funded programs. Small and mid-sized teams need a lighter first step.5

2. Visibility that matches reality

Each decision's gaps roll up into the portfolio view, so the leaders' picture reflects what is really happening.

  • One view of every initiative and its AI type: rules, machine learning, generative AI, agents, vendor features.
  • Gaps are shown per decision, not averaged away.

3. A plan you can act on

  • Prioritised actions, each traced back to a specific initiative and owner.
  • Aligned with the six practices in Australia's Guidance for AI Adoption2 and ASD's AI security guidance.3
  • Export a plain report to share with leaders or your privacy team.

4. Where it fits

The on-ramp before a GRC or runtime platform. Use it to find and size the work; use bigger tools later if you need them.

  1. Find your decisions
  2. See the gaps
  3. Plan and act
  4. Scale up if needed

5. Who it's for

  • Product owners shipping AI features
  • Security and privacy leads
  • Risk and compliance teams
  • Founders and executives

Built for non-finance organisations. It is not designed for banking, superannuation or fintech.

6. Why trust it

  • Free and open source (Apache-2.0)
  • Runs on your device; answers stay in your browser
  • Nothing you enter is captured without your consent
  • Self-host it on your own infrastructure
