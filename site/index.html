    Questions

    What is APP 1.7?

    From 10 December 2026, an APP entity's privacy policy has to describe certain decisions a computer program makes or substantially shapes, where personal information is used and the decision could significantly affect someone's rights or interests. APP 1.8 sets out what that description should cover. See the OAIC fact sheet and Chapter 1 of the APP Guidelines.

    Does it apply to my business?

    It applies to APP entities. Many small businesses under A$3 million annual turnover are exempt unless another test applies, such as being a health service provider, trading in personal information, or being a Commonwealth contracted service provider, or the business opts in. This tool is a screen, not a finding. Enterprise customers may still ask. Credit-like scoring is outside this tool.

    When does it start?

    The transparency obligation starts on 10 December 2026. OAIC's fact sheet describes that date and treats "computer program" broadly, including rule-based processes, AI, machine learning, and other software.

    Do you see my initiative names or answers?

    No. We never see your initiative names or answers. They stay in your browser for this visit and are not stored or sent. This self-hosted edition has no forms that send data.

    What can a penalty look like?

    Infringement notices of up to about A$21,840 per breach for companies may apply (per OAIC). That is a general figure, not a prediction for you. See OAIC infringement notices. This is not legal advice.
